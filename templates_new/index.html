Don't Waste Food. Share It. 🍱

A smart food waste management system that connects hotels with nearby orphanages to donate safe and edible leftover food.

Donate Leftover Food

🍱 Hotel Food Donation

Hotels can register their safe and edible leftover food. Instead of throwing food away, hotels can contact nearby orphanages and arrange a food donation.

📍 Find Nearby Orphanages

Enter the hotel location to find nearby orphanages where safe and edible leftover food can be donated.


🤝 Ready to Donate?

After finding a nearby orphanage, contact them to confirm that they can accept the food. Once confirmed, arrange safe pickup or delivery of the donated food.

How FoodConnect Works

🏨

1. Hotel Has Leftover Food

The hotel identifies safe and edible leftover food that can be donated.

📍

2. Find Nearby Orphanage

The hotel uses Google Maps to locate nearby orphanages.

📞

3. Contact Orphanage

The hotel contacts the orphanage and coordinates the food donation.

🤝

4. Food Is Donated

Safe leftover food reaches people who need it instead of becoming waste.

🍱 About FoodConnect

FoodConnect is a food waste management platform designed to reduce food wastage by connecting hotels with nearby orphanages. The platform helps hotels donate safe and edible leftover food instead of throwing it away.
